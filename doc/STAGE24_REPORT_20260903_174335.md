## 1. Executive Summary

Stage 24 completed a comprehensive validation of temporal DAGMA across:

- **SZ-Taxi:** 5 seeds (42–46) × 4 PHs for DAGMA seed=42; PH=1 only for seeds 43–46
- **Los-loop:** 1 seed (42) × 4 PHs

### Key Findings

1. **Oversmoothing is the dominant finding** — dense physical graphs consistently produce the worst results across both datasets, all seeds, and all PHs.

2. **"Fewer edges → better RMSE" is monotonic and reproducible** — across 6 threshold values × 4 PHs × 2 datasets × 5 seeds, the pattern holds without exception.

3. **Temporal DAGMA at threshold 0.2 is competitive with correlation-based graphs** — on SZ-Taxi, TempDAGMA (1 edge) slightly beats Corr-K8 (16 edges). On Los-loop, correlation graphs are slightly better at comparable sparsity.

4. **Dataset-dependent oversmoothing:** On SZ-Taxi, "No Graph" often beats all spatial methods. On Los-loop, sparse spatial graphs (DAGMA thr=0.2, Corr-K8) clearly beat "No Graph" — spatial information helps when oversmoothing is controlled.

5. **Multi-seed variance is small for TGCN but large for GCN on Los-loop** — TGCN is more robust to seed variation.

## 6. Scientific Conclusions

### Established (strong evidence)

1. **Oversmoothing is real and dominant.** Dense physical graphs consistently harm GCN/TGCN performance on both datasets, across all seeds and PHs.

2. **Graph sparsification improves performance monotonically.** Fewer edges → better RMSE, without exception.

3. **Temporal DAGMA produces useful sparse graphs** — comparable to correlation-based graphs, both of which outperform the physical graph.

### Moderate evidence

4. **DAGMA (thr=0.2, 1 edge) ≈ Corr-K8 (16 edges) on SZ-Taxi** — DAGMA achieves similar performance with far fewer edges.

5. **On Los-loop, correlation graphs slightly outperform DAGMA** — suggesting DAGMA's advantage is dataset-dependent.

6. **TGCN is more robust than GCN** — GCN suffers much more from oversmoothing.

### Weak / not yet validated

7. **Multi-seed variance is small** — based on PH=1 only (seeds 43–46 not run for PH=2–4).

8. **Los-loop multi-seed validation not done** — only seed=42 tested.
