% PART B — WHAT CHANGED vs rev1 (todo5 decisions implemented)
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

1. T-GCN-MultiGSL-Weighted REMOVED from the supplementary matrix.
   - SUPP_VARIANTS in src/supp_paths.py now lists exactly 10 variants
     (5 T-GCN + 5 GCN); --variants choices reject multi_gsl_weighted.
   - Removed from defaults, plans, counts, and aggregator expectations.
   - Canonical references (src/run_canonical_matrix.py, models/multigsl.py,
     src/make_results_figures.py, existing PH1-PH4 results) are UNTOUCHED.
   - If any Weighted file ever appears in a supplementary tree, the
     aggregator reports it as "unexpected" and does NOT aggregate it.

2. Reuse of existing DAGMA results (todo5 #2) — COPY-with-provenance policy:
   - atomic_copy_with_provenance() copies each canonical artifact and writes
     a sidecar JSON: source path, source SHA-256, byte size, copy timestamp,
     dataset/PH/lambda/graph type, and an explicit "source was not modified"
     statement. Source files are opened read-only (verified: checksums
     unchanged after copy).
   - Los-loop gsl PH5/PH6 (lambda 0.02) and all multilag blocks: COPIED,
     never re-fitted. Multilag copies are renamed without PH ambiguity
     (los_multilag_lag1_from_ph1_seed42_lambda002.npy, ...).
   - SZ gsl PH5/PH6 (lambda 0.01): no canonical counterpart -> 2 fresh fits.
   - cGSL: derived from gsl W_est (no fit), saved under the kind's root.
   - No symlinks used.

3. lambda_1 = 0.01 sensitivity fully isolated (todo5 #3):
   - Own root: results/supplementary_ph56_lambda001/ (the two roots mutually
     refuse each other via check_root_safety).
   - λ is enforced by policy: the sensitivity kind hard-requires 0.01
     (explicit --lambda1 0.02 is refused); the completion kind refuses any
     λ different from the dataset's canonical value.
   - Verified wiring: learn_gsl_graph passes the effective lambda straight
     into model.fit(...) and the graph script ASSERTS that the returned fit
     metadata records exactly the requested lambda (a rev1 bug — metadata
     recorded the override while DAGMA got the canonical value — was found
     and fixed in this session's earlier work).

4. Duplicated training removed (todo5 #4):
   - Los-loop T-GCN cells at lambda 0.02 (no_spatial, physical, gsl, cgsl,
     multi_gsl — the 5 variants that exist canonically at PH5/PH6) are
     COPIED from results/stage40_canonical/training/ into
     training_lambda002/ with inline provenance fields (source path, sha256,
     timestamp, "NOT newly trained"). 50 cells.
   - All GCN-family cells and all SZ cells are newly trained (110 cells).
   - Every result JSON carries cell_provenance: newly_trained |
     copied_from_canonical, and the aggregator reports per-cell provenance
     and totals.

5. Updated counts (todo5 #5), verified by the plan printer:
   completion: 2 x 2 x 10 x 5 = 200 logical = 110 newly + 50 copied
               (+ 40 SZ gsl/cgsl cells inside the 110 that wait for the
                SZ graphs; plans show them "blocked" until graphs exist)
   sensitivity: 1 x 2 x 10 x 5 = 100 logical, all newly trained.
